Child Protection First

The 4 Pillars of Nanny Vetting in Sri Lanka

Leaving your young children in someone else's hands requires absolute certainty. Every nanny registered on Nanny.lk undergoes a rigorous 4-stage verification process before being listed.

Note: Nannies provide developmental, supervisory, and basic early childhood care. They are not medical practitioners.

1. National ID & Police Clearance

Verified Grama Niladhari certificate, NIC copy, and Sri Lanka Police background verification.

2. Early Childhood Training

Diplomas or vocational certifications in Childcare, Early Childhood Education, or Nursing Support.

3. Basic First-Aid & CPR Awareness

Certified training in infant choking hazards, basic emergency protocols, and child safety rules.

4. Verified Family References

Direct phone & written reference checks from previous households in Sri Lanka served by the nanny.
